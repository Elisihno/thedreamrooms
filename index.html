THE LOBBY

You've noclipped out of reality. The carpet is damp, the lights hum, and it is very quiet.

Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, tap the figure in the corner to crouch
Take the vase from the table (Space, or tap TAKE) and drop it far away. The thing in the dark goes for it first.
Computer: WASD to move, mouse to look (drag if the cursor isn't captured), arrow keys or Q/E to turn, C to crouch
Find where reality is thin. Listen.
When the lights fail, aim at a table until the hand appears, tap it to hide under, and stay quiet. It only comes if it hears you.

Headphones recommended.
