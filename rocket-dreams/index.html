HOW TO PLAY

  • Hit the ball into the blue goal at the far end. Defend the orange one behind you.
  • Left thumb: drag the stick. Left/right steers, up drives, down reverses.
  • BOOST: hold for a big speed burst. Drive over glowing pads to refill.
  • JUMP: tap to hop. Tap again in the air to flip in the stick direction.
  • In the air the stick tilts your nose. Hold BOOST to fly up for an aerial.
  • DRIFT: hold for a sliding turn. BALL CAM keeps the ball in view.
  • Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space jump, Shift boost, Z drift, C ball cam.
