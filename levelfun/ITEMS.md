# LEVEL FUN — items and what they do

One item can be carried at a time. Look at an item to see its outline, tap the circle (or press E) to pick it up.
DROP appears while you hold something (PC: G drops, right mouse throws). **USE** appears when the item in your hand can do something right now (PC: U, or E when nothing else is targeted). **ZAP** appears when you hold the Party Zapper (PC: F / left mouse).

## Main escape-puzzle items (12 categories)
| # | Item | Where it comes from | Use |
|---|------|---------------------|-----|
| 1 | Exit Key | scattered (Floor 1 rooms) | USE at the padlocked breaker box beside the Emergency Exit (mechanism 1) |
| 2 | Wire Cutters | scattered (basement/storage rooms) | USE at the open junction box beside the exit (mechanism 2); not used up |
| 3 | Exit Code Note (yellow sticky note) | prize of *Find the Party* (arcade) | Hold it up to the exit keypad and tap the hand: the keypad clicks and turns green (mechanism 3). USE elsewhere reads the code |
| 4 | Exit Fuse | scattered (Security, bathrooms, storage) | USE at the fuse cabinet beside the exit (mechanism 4) |
| 5 | Screwdriver | prize of *Pop the Balloons* | USE at the elevator wiring panel (Elevator Room, Floor 3); not used up |
| 6 | Elevator Motor | scattered (generator/boiler/storage) | USE at the winch in the Elevator Room |
| 7 | Elevator Circuit | prize of *Birthday Dash* | USE at the circuit panel in the Elevator Room |
| 8 | Elevator Key | scattered (Floor 3 rooms) | USE at the elevator door in the Maintenance Office once all four repairs are done |
| 9 | Generator Handle | scattered (electrical/boiler/storage) | USE at the basement generator |
| 10 | Power Fuse | scattered (staff/archives/storage/service) | USE at the basement generator; handle + fuse = power repaired |
| 11 | Arcade Token | Prize Counter counter-top | Hold it and tap the hand at an arcade game machine; reusable |
| 12 | Party Stun Gun parts ×3 (Grip, Body, Probe Head) | three different rooms | USE all three at the assembly bench in the Party Preparation Room; the finished Party Stun Gun appears on the bench |

Emergency Exit ending: Exit Key + Wire Cutters + Exit Code Note on the keypad + Exit Fuse, then walk through the open door.
Service Elevator ending: Motor + Circuit + Screwdriver (wiring) + Generator Handle & Power Fuse (power), then the Elevator Key, then walk into the elevator.

## Security cameras
The Security Room (behind the locked door, needs the Turning Handle) has monitors on its east wall. Tap the hand at them to watch all 9 cameras in night vision (PREV / NEXT, EXIT): Floor 1 Main Party Hall, Reception, Dining Room, Kitchen; Floor 2 Arcade, Ball Pit, Indoor Playground; Floor 3 Abandoned Celebration Hall, Maintenance Corridor.
